## 中文

### 激光信任是什么

激光信任（Jiguang Trust）是一个面向普通用户和项目方的链上信任与交易平台：交易前先查合约风险，再用自己的钱包完成操作。

### 非托管架构

- 平台不保存私钥或助记词，不代用户签名。
- 每一笔链上操作都由用户在自己的钱包里确认签名。
- 平台不托管用户资产，不自动转账或提现。

| 同链闪兑 | 自己钱包签名，手续费 0.25%（与 PancakeSwap V2 持平） |

## English

### What is Jiguang Trust

Jiguang Trust is an onchain trust and trading platform for users and token projects: check a contract's risk before you trade, then act with your own wallet.

### Non-custodial by design

- The platform never stores private keys or seed phrases and never signs on your behalf.
- Every onchain action is confirmed and signed by you in your own wallet.
- No custody of user assets, no automatic transfers or withdrawals.

### Live

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Score | Token contract risk checks and trust score |
| Market | Token market data and radar boards |
| One-click token launch on BSC mainnet | Template-based launch, deployed and signed from your own wallet |
| Same-chain swap | Signed in your own wallet; 0.25% fee, on par with PancakeSwap V2 |

### Coming soon

- Cross-chain
- Self-built liquidity pools
- Quant subscriptions for projects (four tiers: 199U / 299U / 699U / 1999U per month)
